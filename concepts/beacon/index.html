Rare is a countdown.

One million species are threatened with extinction.1 Monitored wildlife populations have fallen 73% on average since 1970.2 These are ten of the faces.

Fall 2026 Community Release

EcoSurge opens up. The code is public, the data is open, and this season we are asking people to help build it: naturalists, developers, makers and anyone who can organize a Saturday.

Sep 22 · Equinox8 weeks of fall leftDec 21 · Solstice
Today

What ships in Release 01

  1. Archive

    The Species Archive. 3,218 species across the tree of life, 14 biomes, 3D globes and clade diagrams, open for corrections and new records.

    Live
  2. Watch

    Fire Watch and the feed pattern behind it. Poll a public instrument, fall back to a snapshot, and never show a fake zero when a feed is down.

    Live
  3. Zoo

    The structure zoo. 50 molecules and living structures, from DNA to ATP synthase, rendered in the browser.

    Preview
  4. Bench

    Open hardware. Parametric blueprints for the Zen Gardener's deck, camera mast and LIDAR pedestal, and a stair-sweeper physics lab with ten concepts.

    Prototype

Four ways in.

Pick the one that fits your hands. Every track ends in something real: a record, a feed, a part, a field day.

  1. 01Naturalists

    Add a species

    Correct a record, add a range, attach a field photo. Every species page is one pull request away.

  2. 02Developers

    Wire a feed

    Storms, quakes, solar weather. Bring a public instrument into the Watch, built the Fire Watch way.

  3. 03Makers

    Print a part

    Print the deck, mount the mast, run the stair sim. Send back what broke; that is the useful part.

  4. 04Organizers

    Host a field day

    A cleanup, a planting, a bioblitz. Bring your town; we will help you log what you find.

Community engagement, all season.

  1. In personField days. Cleanups, plantings and bioblitzes, hosted in your town.
  2. OnlineSpecies sprints. A weekend, a clade, and everyone filling it in together.
  3. WorkshopOpen bench. Build nights for the robots, with the sims running beside them.
  4. LiveRelease call. A walkthrough of what shipped and what the community changed.

Dates go out to the list first.

03 / 05The beaconDivisions

What the beacon lights.

Green tech is scattered across labs, farms and garages. EcoSurge puts it in one place, in public, where it can be checked and built on.

  1. 01

    Archive

    Live

    The tree of life from domain to species: field guides, clade diagrams, biome dashboards and 3D globes.

  2. 02

    Watch

    Live

    Planetary feeds from public instruments. Fire Watch runs on NASA EONET today; storms, disasters and trends come next.

  3. 03

    Field

    Building

    Agriculture in harmony with nature. Farms hold half the world's habitable land,9 so that is where the lever is.

  4. 04

    Machines

    Prototype

    Biomechanical robots: reflexes on the robot, perception on the desktop. The Zen Gardener is on the bench.

  5. 05

    Genome

    Research

    Biotechnology, starting with sight: the structures life is built from, rendered so anyone can see them.
